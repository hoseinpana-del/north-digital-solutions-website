Technology

The right tool for the problem — not the other way round

Where a business already runs on Microsoft, the Power Platform is often the fastest route: Power Apps for data entry, Power Automate for workflow, Power BI for dashboards, Dataverse or SQL underneath.

Where it isn’t the right fit — or where the need is a product rather than a project — we build the software ourselves. bari is that path: a full platform, built in-house, running on its own database and access control.

We assess the requirement, the number of users and the data sources first, then choose the technology and the licensing. The aim is to avoid paying for tools a business doesn’t actually need.
